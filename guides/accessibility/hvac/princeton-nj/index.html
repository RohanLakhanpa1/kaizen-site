Website accessibility checklist for hvac businesses in Princeton, NJ

A plain-English checklist for hvac owners in Princeton, NJ. Accessible sites are easier for everyone to use, including customers on phones, older customers, and people who use screen readers or keyboards.

  1. Maintenance-plan sign-up. Label every field and don't rely on placeholder text alone.
  2. Seasonal promo banners. Offers that exist only as images are invisible to screen readers.
  3. Service-area maps. Add the towns you serve as text next to the map.
  4. Images. Meaningful images need alt text; purely decorative ones should be marked so they're skipped.
  5. Colour contrast. Body text should have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
  6. Forms. Each field needs a programmatic label, and errors must be described in text.
  7. Keyboard use. Every menu, button and form must work with Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter, with a visible focus outline.
  8. Page structure. One h1, headings in order, and a descriptive page title.
