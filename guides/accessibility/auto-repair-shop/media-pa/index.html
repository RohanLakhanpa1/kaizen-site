Website accessibility checklist for auto repair shop businesses in Media, PA

A plain-English checklist for auto repair shop owners in Media, PA. Accessible sites are easier for everyone to use, including customers on phones, older customers, and people who use screen readers or keyboards.

  1. Appointment forms. Label every field and make sure the date picker works without a mouse.
  2. Coupon and special-offer images. Offers that exist only as images are invisible to screen readers.
  3. Service lists. Use real headings so people using assistive tech can jump between services.
  4. Images. Meaningful images need alt text; purely decorative ones should be marked so they're skipped.
  5. Colour contrast. Body text should have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
  6. Forms. Each field needs a programmatic label, and errors must be described in text.
  7. Keyboard use. Every menu, button and form must work with Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter, with a visible focus outline.
  8. Page structure. One h1, headings in order, and a descriptive page title.

What is WCAG?

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines are the widely used technical standard for accessible websites. Most checklists, including this one, are based on WCAG 2.1 level AA.
