How it works

Grade appeal pack

What we ask you for: Name, Course, Instructor, Grade received, What you think is wrong, What you ask.

What you get: A respectful grade review email, a short meeting request, a one-page summary of the case and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Professor email pack

What we ask you for: Name, Course and instructor, What you need, Your situation in brief, What you are prepared to do, Contact.

What you get: An email to a professor, a short version, a follow-up and a meeting-request version.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
