How it works

Penalty abatement request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Office, What the penalty is, Why the penalty happened, What you are asking for, Contact.

What you get: A request to remove or reduce a tax penalty, a phone script, a document checklist and a follow-up.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Tax notice response pack

What we ask you for: Name, Notice from, What it says, Your response facts, What you want, Contact.

What you get: A response letter to the office on your notice, a phone script and a mailing checklist.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
