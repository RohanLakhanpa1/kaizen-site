How it works

Sitter instruction sheet pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who is being looked after, The routine, House rules, Contact, Tone.

What you get: A one-page sitter instruction sheet, a welcome text, a routine card and a what-to-do-if checklist.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

School concern letter pack

What we ask you for: Name, Child first name and grade, School or teacher, Concern details, What you ask, Contact.

What you get: A calm email to a teacher or school, a follow-up, a meeting script, three replies to the usual answers and a dated log.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
