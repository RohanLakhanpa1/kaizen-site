How it works

Raise request pack: a meeting request, a one-page case from your own achievements, a follo

What we ask you for: Your name, Your job title, Your manager's name, How long you've been in the role, Your current pay, The pay you want.

What you get: A meeting request email, a one-page case built from your own achievements, a follow-up email, three replies to usual pushback, the increase and percentage worked out and a preparation checklist.

We only use what you type. We will not invent an achievement, a market rate or a competing offer, and we write no ultimatum. We work out the increase and percentage for you.

Performance review & write-up pack: a review narrative and, if needed, a neutral incident

What we ask you for: What this person does well, as specific observations, Specific areas to improve, with examples if you have them.

What you get: A performance review narrative, and, if an incident was reported, a neutral incident write-up for the file.

We only use what you type. We will not invent an observation, an incident, or a rating, and we refuse to include any protected-characteristic language.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
