How it works

Back in stock pack: a waitlist email, a text and a social post for a restocked or returnin

What we ask you for: Your business name, The item, Date it is or will be available, YYYY-MM-DD, Link to buy it, starting with https.

What you get: A back-in-stock waitlist email, a short text and a social post.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a quantity, price or deadline, and we say it is available only on or after your date.

Upsell follow-up pack: a post-purchase thank-you with one related offer, a text, a follow-

What we ask you for: Your business name, What the customer just bought, The ONE related thing you want to offer, in a sentence, Price of the related offer, for example $49, Link where they can get it, starting with https.

What you get: A post-purchase offer email, a short text, a follow-up, the computed send date and, if you gave your numbers, the arithmetic on them.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a benefit, discount or deadline. Any estimate is arithmetic on the numbers you give, not a forecast.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
