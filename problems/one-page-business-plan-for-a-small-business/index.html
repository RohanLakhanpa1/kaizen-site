How it works

What you get: A one-page business plan, a short summary for a lender or partner and a list of questions still to answer.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Grant application draft: answers to your funder's questions from your own facts, plus a li

What we ask you for: Organization name, Paste the funder's application questions, one per line, Your mission and a short history, as you would state them, The need or problem you address, with any real numbers and where they come from, The program or project you want funded.
