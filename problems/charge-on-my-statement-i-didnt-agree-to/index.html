How it works

Bank or card charge dispute pack

What we ask you for: Name, Bank or card, Merchant, Charge amount, Charge date, What is wrong.

What you get: A written dispute to the bank or card issuer, a note to the merchant, a phone script and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Subscription cancel pack: a written cancellation request, a follow-up and, if you were cha

What we ask you for: Your name, The company and what the subscription is, Your account, member number or the email on the account, Where to send the request, Days you give them to confirm in writing, How they can reach you.

What you get: A written cancellation request, a follow-up email, the confirmation-due date, a table and total of charges after cancelling, a note for the bank when there are such charges, and an evidence checklist.

We only use what you type. We remove long numbers like card numbers. We will not cite a law, promise a refund you didn't ask for, or threaten anyone, because rights depend on where you live and what you signed.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
