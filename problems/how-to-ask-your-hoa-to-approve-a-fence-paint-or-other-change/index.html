How it works

HOA change approval request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Association or manager, What you want to change, Where on the property, Contact.

What you get: A request to an HOA to approve a change, a short neighbor note and a follow-up.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

HOA notice response pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who sent the notice, What the notice says, Your side, What you ask, Contact.

What you get: A calm reply to a notice from an HOA or landlord, a request for details in writing, a phone script and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
