How it works

Recommendation letter pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who you are recommending, How you know them, What you saw them do, What the letter is for, Contact.

What you get: A recommendation letter, a short version for an online form and a reply to the person who asked.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Reference request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Referee, Relationship, Applying for, Contact.

What you get: A polite reference request email, a one-paragraph summary for the referee and a thank-you note.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
