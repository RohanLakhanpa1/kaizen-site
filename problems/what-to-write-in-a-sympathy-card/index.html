How it works

Sympathy message pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who you are writing to, Who has died, Memories you have, Tone, Contact.

What you get: A sympathy card message, a short text, a longer letter and a list of things to avoid saying.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Eulogy pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who they were to you, Their name, Memories you have, Tone, Contact.

What you get: A eulogy in two lengths from the person's own memories, alternative opening and closing lines and a delivery card.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
