What we ask you for: Organization name, Paste the funder's application questions, one per line, Your mission and a short history, as you would state them, The need or problem you address, with any real numbers and where they come from, The program or project you want funded.

What you get: Answers to each of the funder's questions (up to 8) within your word limits, and a list of what is still missing; anything we cannot know is left blank for you to fill in.

We only use what you type. We will not invent outcomes, statistics, partners, awards or tax status; where something is missing the draft says [ORG TO ADD] and lists it for you.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
