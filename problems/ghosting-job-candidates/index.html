How it works

Candidate update pack: a short, professional email to every candidate on your list, from t

What we ask you for: The role they applied for, One candidate per line.

What you get: One short email per candidate on the list, grouped by status.

We only use the status and reason you give for each candidate. We will not invent a reason, and we refuse any candidate whose reason mentions a protected characteristic.

Hiring pack: job post, screening and interview questions, and a first-week checklist, from

What we ask you for: Business name, Job title, Location, or remote/hybrid, What the person will do day to day, What they must have or be able to do to do the job.

What you get: A job post, 6 screening questions, 8 interview questions with what to listen for, and a first-week checklist; anything we cannot know is left blank for you to fill in.

We only use what you type. We will not invent pay, perks or benefits, and we cannot write requirements about age, sex, family status, religion, nationality or disability.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
