How it works

Refund/return reply pack: a customer-facing reply and an internal note, from your own retu

What we ask you for: Your return/refund policy, as you would state it, What the customer is asking for and why, in their words or yours.

What you get: A customer-facing reply and an internal note, with a computed return-window date check where dates were given.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a policy term, a reason, or whether this request is inside your window -- the date check is arithmetic on the dates you give.

Draft replies to your customer reviews

What we ask you for: Business name, Paste your reviews.

What you get: Draft replies to up to 30 pasted reviews, sensitive ones flagged.

Up to 30 reviews. Paste exactly what customers wrote. We reply only to what is written; we will not invent details about what happened.

Store policy pack: a returns and refunds policy, a shipping policy and a checkout notice,

What we ask you for: Your business name, What you sell, in a sentence, How many days after delivery customers can return an item, How customers contact you.

What you get: A returns and refunds policy, a shipping policy, a one-line checkout notice, a table of the seller's terms and a list of undecided topics.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a return window, fee, guarantee or legal right, and anything you leave blank is listed for you to decide.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
