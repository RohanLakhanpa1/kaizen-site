How it works

Credit report dispute pack

What we ask you for: Name, Which bureau, The item in question, Why you say it is wrong, What you ask, Contact.

What you get: A credit report dispute letter, a follow-up, a document checklist and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Bank or card charge dispute pack

What we ask you for: Name, Bank or card, Merchant, Charge amount, Charge date, What is wrong.

What you get: A written dispute to the bank or card issuer, a note to the merchant, a phone script and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
