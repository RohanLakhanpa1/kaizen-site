How it works

Workplace change request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Employer, What makes the work hard, What would help, Contact.

What you get: A written workplace change request, a meeting script, a follow-up and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Work flexibility request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Role, Manager, What you ask, How work stays covered, Contact.

What you get: A meeting request, a one-page proposal for remote days or a schedule change, four replies to the usual pushback and a follow-up.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
