How it works

Public records request pack

What you get: A written public records request, a follow-up and a tracking sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

We only use what you type. We will not decide what you must disclose, cite any law's section number, or tell the requester their request is invalid. The due date is arithmetic; extensions are not applied.
