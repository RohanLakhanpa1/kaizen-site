How it works

Debt verification request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Collector, What you think the debt is, Contact.

What you get: A letter asking a collector to put the debt details in writing, a call script, a contact log and a follow-up.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Goodwill letter pack

What we ask you for: Name, Creditor, Account ending, What you are asking for, What happened, Contact.

What you get: A goodwill letter asking a creditor to remove a late mark, a phone script, a follow-up and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
