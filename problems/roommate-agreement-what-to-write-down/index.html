How it works

Roommate agreement pack

What we ask you for: Name, Who lives there, House rules, Shared costs and chores, Tone, Contact.

What you get: A household agreement, a message to bring it up, a chore and cost table and check-in questions.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Deposit return pack: a demand letter for your security deposit, a follow-up email, the amo

What we ask you for: Your name, Landlord or property manager's name, The rental address, Date you moved out, YYYY-MM-DD, Where to send the money or reply, Deposit you paid.

What you get: A demand letter for the security deposit, a follow-up email, the amount requested worked out, the deadline countdown and an evidence checklist.

We only use what you type. We work out the amount you're asking for. We will not quote a law, a penalty, a multiplier or threaten court, because those depend on where you live: check your lease and your local rules.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
