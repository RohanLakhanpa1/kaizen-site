How it works

Ticket appeal pack: a polite written appeal for a parking or traffic ticket, a deadline co

What we ask you for: Your name, Citation or ticket number, exactly as printed, Date on the ticket, YYYY-MM-DD, Where it was issued, Fine shown on the ticket, Who issued it and where it says to send an appeal.

What you get: A polite written appeal for a parking or traffic ticket, a computed deadline countdown and timeline, an evidence checklist for the driver's grounds and a list of things to check.

We only use what you type. We will not admit the offence, cite a law, describe a sign you didn't, or accuse the officer or the city. We work out your deadline from the date you read off the ticket.

Bill and denial appeal pack: draft appeal or dispute letters for a medical bill, insurance

What we ask you for: What is it about?, Who are you writing to?, What happened.

What you get: A markdown document built only from your facts; anything we cannot know is left blank for you to fill in.

The letters use only what you type. Dates, amounts and reference numbers you leave out are marked for you to add.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
