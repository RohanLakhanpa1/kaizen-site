How it works

Unemployment appeal pack

What we ask you for: Name, Office, What was decided, Reason given, Why you disagree, Contact.

What you get: An unemployment appeal letter, talking points for a hearing and an evidence checklist.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Benefits denial appeal pack

What we ask you for: Name, Benefit type, Office, What was denied, Reason given, Why you disagree.

What you get: A benefits appeal letter, a request for the reasons in writing, a phone script and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
