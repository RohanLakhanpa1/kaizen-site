How it works

Payment plan request pack: a letter proposing a plan, a phone script, a written-confirmati

What we ask you for: Your name, Who you owe, The balance you owe, for example $1,800, What you can pay each month, for example $150, Date of your first payment, YYYY-MM-DD, How the creditor should reach you.

What you get: A letter or email proposing a payment plan, a phone script, a follow-up asking for the plan in writing, three replies to the usual answers, and every payment date and amount worked out.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a hardship story, a legal right, a promise or a threat, and we ask for no interest or fee waiver unless you say so. We work out every payment date and check the payments add up.

Bill and denial appeal pack: draft appeal or dispute letters for a medical bill, insurance

What we ask you for: What is it about?, Who are you writing to?, What happened.

What you get: A markdown document built only from your facts; anything we cannot know is left blank for you to fill in.

The letters use only what you type. Dates, amounts and reference numbers you leave out are marked for you to add.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
