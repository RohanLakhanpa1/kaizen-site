How it works

Medical bill review request pack

What we ask you for: Name, Provider or billing office, What the bill says, What looks wrong, What you ask, Contact.

What you get: A request for an itemised bill and a review, a follow-up, a phone script, three replies to the usual answers and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Insurance claim appeal pack

What we ask you for: Name, Insurer, Claim number, What was denied, Reason given, Why you disagree.

What you get: A written claim appeal, a request for the reasons in writing, a phone script and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
