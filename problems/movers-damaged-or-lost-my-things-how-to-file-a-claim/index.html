How it works

Moving company claim pack

What we ask you for: Name, Moving company, What was damaged or lost, Move date, What you want, Contact.

What you get: A written claim for damaged or missing items from a move, a follow-up, a phone script and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Carrier claim pack: a lost or damaged shipment claim letter, a filing-deadline countdown a

What we ask you for: Your business name, Carrier and service, What happened, Tracking number, Date you shipped it, as YYYY-MM-DD, How many days after shipping the carrier allows a claim to be filed.

What you get: A claim letter to the carrier, a computed filing-deadline countdown, an exhibit list and a paperwork checklist.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a tracking number, value, date or evidence. The filing deadline is arithmetic on the window you give us: check your carrier's own page for the real one.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
