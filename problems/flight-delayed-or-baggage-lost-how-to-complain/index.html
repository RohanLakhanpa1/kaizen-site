How it works

Travel complaint pack

What we ask you for: Name, Company, Trip details, What went wrong, What you want, Contact.

What you get: A travel complaint email, a follow-up, a phone or chat script and three replies to the usual answers.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Refund request pack

What we ask you for: Your name, Who you bought from, Amount you paid, for example $60, Purchase date, What went wrong, in your own words, How they should reach you.

What you get: A first refund request email, a firmer escalation email, a chat or phone script, replies to the usual answers and the follow-up dates worked out.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
