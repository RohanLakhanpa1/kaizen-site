How it works

Contractor complaint pack

What we ask you for: Name, Contractor or company, What was agreed, What went wrong, What you want, Contact.

What you get: A letter about unfinished or poor work, a follow-up, a phone script and a record sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Warranty claim reply pack: a customer-facing reply and an internal note, from your warrant

What we ask you for: Your warranty terms, as you would state them, What the customer says is wrong, in their words or yours.

What you get: A customer-facing reply and an internal note, with a computed warranty-window date check where dates were given.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a coverage term or whether this claim is inside your warranty length -- the date check is arithmetic on the dates you give.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
