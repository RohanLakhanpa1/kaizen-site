How it works

Supplier price pushback pack: a supplier email, phone talking points, and a computed numbe

What we ask you for: What you buy from them, Your current price per unit, in dollars, Their proposed new price per unit, in dollars.

What you get: A computed numbers sheet (percent increase, yearly cost, optional margin impact, phased schedule), a supplier email, and phone talking points.

We only use what you type. The percent, yearly cost, margin and phased prices are arithmetic on your own numbers. We will not invent a competitor quote, threaten to leave, or promise a discount.

Price increase pack: customer email, short notice, replies to pushback and staff talking p

What we ask you for: Business name, What is changing, Current price or range, New price or range, Date the new price starts.

What you get: A customer email, a short notice, 5 replies to pushback and staff talking points; anything we cannot know is left blank for you to fill in.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a reason, a discount or a deadline. Leave a field blank and the draft marks the gap for you.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
