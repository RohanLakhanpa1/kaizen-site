How it works

Neighbor or HOA complaint pack

What we ask you for: Name, Other party, Problem, Since when, What you want, Contact.

What you get: A calm first note to the neighbor, a written follow-up, a letter to the HOA or manager and a dated log sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Repair request pack

What we ask you for: Your name, Who must make the repair, What is wrong, in your own words, Date the problem started, How they should reach you.

What you get: A written first repair request, a follow-up, a final written notice, a phone script, the dates worked out and a contact log sheet.

We only use what you type. We will not invent a fact, a figure, a law, a deadline, a promise or a threat, and we promise no result.

Your draft is written by AI from the facts and files you give us, and we use nothing else. The owner reviews each new kind of order, and orders of a kind that has been approved several times are released after a short review window. Read everything before you send or use it.
